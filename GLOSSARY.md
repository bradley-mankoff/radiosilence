# RadioSilence

RadioSilence (called Bad Game Warning until 2026-11-14) tells a fan whether a game is worth watching from behind, without spoiling it. These are the words the project uses for that.

## Watching

**Watching from behind**:
Starting a game after tip-off, often at halftime or later, and catching up by skipping the commercials.
_Avoid_: delayed viewing, DVR viewing

**Spoiler**:
Anything that tells the fan more about how a game is going than the Verdict does, such as the score, clock, quarter, a win-probability percentage, or a highlight title that names the winner.
_Avoid_: leak

**Game day**:
The local calendar day on which a game tips off; its Verdict stays up until midnight.

## Judging a game

**Verdict**:
The one answer the page gives about a game: Watch, Stay away or Too early to call.
_Avoid_: score, rating, recommendation

**Watch**:
The Verdict for a game that is still worth watching.

**Stay away**:
The Verdict for a game that is out of reach for the Mavericks.
_Avoid_: don't watch, skip, bad game, call it off

**Too early to call**:
The Verdict for a game that has tipped off but has not yet reached the Judging window.

**Reading**:
One win-probability value that ESPN publishes after a play.
_Avoid_: data point, sample, tick

**Stay away line**:
The opponent's win probability above which a game counts as out of reach.
_Avoid_: threshold, cutoff, odds

**Judging window**:
The part of a game in which the Stay away line applies, from 6:00 left in the 2nd quarter to the end.
_Avoid_: cutoff

**Preset**:
One named kind of game a fan likes to watch, such as wins for my team, close games or high-scoring games. A fan picks any number of Presets and chooses whether a game must fit all of them (AND) or any one (OR) to read Watch.
_Avoid_: filter, mode, profile

The Presets that ship are **Wins for my team**, **No blowout losses**, **Close games** and **High-scoring games**. A fan's edits to a Preset's settings overwrite its shipped definition, and Reset to default restores it.

**Close ending**:
A game that was within 5 points at some moment in the last 5:00 of the 4th quarter. It is enough on its own for Close games.

**Close along the way**:
A game that was within 9 points for more than 66% of its time. Close games falls back to it when there is no Close ending.

**Tracked player**:
A player a fan follows instead of, or as well as, a team, judged on the end-of-game numbers the fan sets, and projected during the game by the share of the player's usual minutes already played.

**Catch-up view**:
The box score or play-by-play as of the quarter and clock the fan sets, opened by the I'm watching button and rebuilt from the saved play-by-play, never showing anything later.

## When no game is on

**Next-game preview**:
The day, time and opponent of the next game, shown when no game has tipped off on the Game day.

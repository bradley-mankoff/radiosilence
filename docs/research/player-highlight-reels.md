## 4. Title grammar and why stat lines and existence both spoil

League titles put the player first and the game second in free text, with the stat line always present and the date always at the end after a pipe: Darryn Peterson CAREER-HIGH 28 PTS vs Rockets | November 13, 2026, Franz Wagner was UNSTOPPABLE with 35 PTS vs Bucks | November 13, 2026, Sengun Makes Rockets HISTORY with 40 plus PTS, 10 plus REB and 5 plus AST on 70 percent plus shooting | November 13, 2026, Trae Young GOES OFF for 37 PTS and 14 AST vs. Pistons | November 12, 2026, Wemby Powers Spurs To 10th Straight Win With 32 PTS, 5 BLK At Chicago | November 11, 2026, and the same-game pair Cooper Flagg (43 PTS, 10 REB, 6 AST) vs. Indiana Pacers | November 9, 2026 on the team channel versus Cooper Flagg Drops 43 PTS Now No. 3 All-Time in NBA Points Before Turning 20 | November 9, 2026 on the league channel, which proves the same performance gets two different titles and two different video IDs (EQQ6PWdio4E on @mavericks, dSHvpoQQKzY on @NBA). Team titles are more regular but equally spoilery: Player Name, parenthesized or bare stat line, versus or at plus opponent, pipe plus full date, with case and punctuation varying between 35 points and 35 PTS, between vs. and vs, and with at least one malformed date in October 30 2026, so matching must normalize case, punctuation, PTS versus points, REB versus rebounds, AST versus assists, and the optional comma. Single-play clips such as SGA with the tough side step triple for 40 PTS or Jared McCain from near the logo at the buzzer share the uploads list and mention points, so duration plus playlist membership is the only way to separate a 2 to 9 minute one-game reel from a seconds-long single play. Spoiler analysis follows directly: every per-player title carries points and often rebounds, assists, blocks, or history language such as CAREER-HIGH, HISTORY, UNSTOPPABLE, GOES OFF, Takeover, Masterclass, and No. 3 All-Time, so rendering any title, thumbnail, view count, or duration spoils the Tracked player night, and the page must store the video ID but display only a plain label. Existence analysis splits by source: on the league channel only 4 player reels cover the 9 full-game reels on November 13, 2026, only 2 reels cover the 4 full-game reels on November 12, 2026 and both are from the same Pistons at Wizards 2OT game, and only 3 reels cover the 8 full-game reels on November 11, 2026, so an NBA-channel reel means a big night and its presence is itself a Spoiler. On the team channel 3 spotlights plus a Highlights reel is the common shape even for role players, so presence alone says little, but the November 4 and November 12 gaps show absence is not yet fully predictable either.
